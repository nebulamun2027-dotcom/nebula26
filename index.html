The Conference

Where ideas take the floor, and stand.

NEBULA MUN is a two-day Model United Nations conference in Meerut where delegates move through the full arc of diplomacy: rigorous research, sharp negotiation, persuasive public speaking and the calm authority of principled leadership. It is a floor built for the position paper that lands, the amendment that holds, and the voice that carries a room of one hundred strangers.

Over two days, eight committees carry delegates from clause to clause and crisis to resolution. Come prepared to research deep, argue clean and negotiate under pressure: because on the NEBULA floor, the briefest sentence in the room is often the one that changes the outcome.

01

Diplomacy

Build bridges one clause at a time, and turn strangers into co-sponsors.

02

Negotiation

Trade, hold, close. Learn where a position bends and where it must not.

03

Research

Evidence is ammunition. The briefest, best-researched line usually wins the point.

04

Public Speaking

Find the voice that holds a hundred delegates through a full session.

05

Leadership

Lead a bloc, a resolution, a room. Influence is earned in public, one decision at a time.
